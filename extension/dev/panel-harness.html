4 U By U — the panel, on a stand-in lecture

This page loads the same files the userscript does (/src/*.js) and gives the panel a stand-in: a made-up 45-minute lecture, a stand-in API key, and a stand-in provider that answers in the shape Groq does. Nothing here reaches the network, and no key is needed — so the whole thing can be driven, watched and repeated at no cost.

Drive it

The panel is installed exactly as the userscript installs it, with its button in the bottom-right corner. Open it, paste any text as the key, and press Check the key and list models.
